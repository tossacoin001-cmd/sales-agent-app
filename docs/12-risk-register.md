# Risk register

| Risk | Signal | Control / stop condition | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| No willingness to pay | Praise without paid commitment or renewal | Narrow job or stop before build | Product |
| Activity mistaken for value | Screening/sends grow; decisions do not | Track time, usefulness and decisions | Product |
| Weak contact sources | Repeated failures despite MX | Hold and investigate; no address cycling | Operations |
| Duplicate external send | Timeout, restart, concurrent worker | Durable intent and unknown-state reconciliation | Engineering |
| False diagnosis | Confident cause from silence or minor typo | Facts/hypotheses separated; review | Product/AI |
| Tenant data leak | Wrong workspace in job, cache or retrieval | RLS plus app checks and adversarial tests | Security |
| Mailbox-access distrust | Buyers refuse broad permission | Narrow manual pilot and clear purpose | Product |
| Provider approval delay | Unbudgeted restricted-scope review | Verify requirements early | Engineering |
| Hidden labour cost | Support grows per customer | Measure labour; assisted fee or redesign | Founder |
| Global overreach | Too many channels/markets too early | One cohort; native currency per goal | Product |
| Misstated cash | Invoice or claim counted as receipt | Evidence basis, settlement and reversals | Finance |
| Goal pressure drives unsafe action | Volume or approval bypass | Hard limits and no income guarantees | Product |
| Prompt injection | External content asks for actions | Data boundary and tool allowlist | Security |
| Stale records shown as live | Expired watch, quota or cursor | Last sync/coverage and write pause | Engineering |
| Channel policy breach | Unauthorised messaging | Official supported adapters and consent | Operations |
| Product fragmentation | OS and new app duplicate work | Repository audit and shared foundation | Founder |
| False attribution | All customer money credited to app | Direct, assisted and unknown labels | Analytics |
| False test confidence | Offline pass used as sales proof | State limits and require a real pilot | Product |

Any critical privacy, permission, financial-integrity or external-action incident pauses writes immediately. A sales deadline never overrides the controls.
