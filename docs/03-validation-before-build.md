# Validate before building
## Principle
“Proven” must identify the claim, evidence, cohort and period. Workflow logic may be tested offline. Customer value requires real users and actual behaviour. Revenue uplift requires stronger outcome evidence. Do not substitute a synthetic simulation, owner praise or competitor existence for demand.

## Stage0 — completed desk checks
Case audit, competitive research,33offline assertions and assumed-cost sensitivity. Outcome:conditional proceed to discovery; application build not approved by evidence.

## Stage1 — ten problem interviews, one narrow cohort
Recruit only with explicit permission for research outreach; this document does not authorise messages. Start with opted-in existing relationships, not suppressed sprint prospects. No sensitive mailbox access required for an interview.

Ask about a recent actual enquiry or quote:
1. Walk through the last opportunity that became difficult to manage.
2. Which tools were used, and who handled each step?
3. What exactly was missed, delayed or duplicated?
4. How did you discover it? What evidence exists?
5. What workaround do you use, and how much time does it take?
6. Which parts of your existing CRM/WhatsApp/shop already work well?
7. What would you refuse to let software do without approval?
8. What would need to happen for this to be worth paying for?
9. Who would approve a subscription and give data-access permission?
10. Would you test a narrowly described assisted service with your current opportunities?

Record consent, date, segment, verbatim evidence privately, problem frequency, current cost, authority and willingness to participate. Do not turn hypothetical enthusiasm into intent to buy.

Proposed gate:at least6/10 describe a recurring relevant problem with a recent concrete example; at least4 agree to a consented workflow walkthrough; at least3 make a specific commitment to a pilot. Thresholds are decision rules, not statistical market proof. If findings split by segment, choose a narrower cohort before proceeding.

## Stage2 — usability and comprehension test
Create a clearly labelled clickable or slide prototype only after interviews. Five target users, without coaching:
- After five seconds, explain what it does and who it is for.
- Enter a realistic goal and understand cash versus invoiced value.
- Find the most important actual next action and its evidence.
- Approve/edit a draft and identify actions the tool cannot perform.
- Interpret “last synced” and “delivery unconfirmed.”
Proposed gate:4/5correctly explain value;4/5complete the primary task without a facilitator; no user mistakes an invoice for verified cash or a draft for a sent message. Any critical misunderstanding requires redesign even if the headline scores pass.

## Stage3 — consented concierge pilot before a SaaS
Five businesses in the same cohort. Proposed duration:14 days baseline followed by30 days assisted use, then renewal decision at the actual billing period. No new custom app or paid ads required. Use current approved tools, manual review and private customer-specific reports.
- Customer nominates one business goal, one offer and a real existing opportunity pipeline.
- Identify agreed records and purpose. Prefer narrow customer-provided data initially; do not collect whole mailboxes by default.
- Record baseline follow-up coverage, response times, review time and confirmed outcomes.
- Human operator reconciles evidence, presents at most3 priority actions, prepares drafts and records actual customer decisions.
- Owner approves every external message and commercial scope during the pilot.
- No automatic bank details, discounts, contracts or unapproved campaigns.
- Unsupported channels remain prepared/manual. Owner records an action as done; software cannot infer it.
- All customer data remains separated; no public case study without consent.

## Primary pilot metrics
Definitions must be fixed before starting:
- Activation:one valid goal + permitted data source + correctly linked opportunity + first chosen action within48h.
- Review time:measured minutes/week using the prior method versus pilot, including time supplying data.
- Follow-up coverage:eligible opportunities with a completed/reviewed next step divided by all eligible opportunities at the agreed cadence.
- Recommendation usefulness:owner marks accept/edit/reject with reason; acceptance is not completion.
- Completion:actions evidenced as executed within their due window.
- Accuracy:human audit of classification, evidence links and financial totals.
- Retention:meaningful use in each of4 weeks; not just logins.
- Willingness to pay:actual agreed pilot payment and an unpressured renewal, not “I would pay.”
- Outcome:qualified decisions, written scopes and provider/bank-verified receipts, reported separately.
- Incidents:wrong recipient, duplicate dispatch, unauthorised disclosure, opt-out contact, incorrect cash total.

## Proposed build gate
At least4/5 activate; at least3/5 use weekly across4 weeks; at least3/5 pay at the stated test price; at least2 renew without bespoke persuasion.
At least3/5show either ≥30%less review time or ≥20percentage-point better follow-up coverage against baseline, with no fall in relevant conversation quality.
Financial ledger matches the agreed audited sample100%; critical execution/privacy incidents0.
Direct recurring costs including support fit a credible ≥75%gross-margin model at the planned service level.
These thresholds are proposed and should be fixed before recruitment. Small cohorts provide directional evidence only.

Revenue lift is not required to prove the narrower “saves time and improves follow-up discipline” claim. It IS required before selling it as a proven revenue generator. Record opportunity mix, lead source, seasonality, owner effort and other concurrent changes. With5 businesses and short timelines, no causal revenue claim is justified. Use matched cohorts or randomisation later if claiming incremental outcomes.

## Pricing test
Present one clear offer to comparable participants; record actual acceptance and renewal. Initial test anchors are ₦15k/month solo and ₦35k/month small team, subject to scope/cost results; not approved public launch prices. Avoid showing multiple confusing menus or changing prices mid-pilot without recording it. A labour-heavy concierge may need a separately disclosed fee; don't hide its cost inside a pretend scalable margin.

## Stop/pivot conditions
No recurring pain, low data-access trust, low usage, no paid commitment or high support cost means revise segment/workflow rather than add features.
If users value a weekly human review more than daily software, sell an assisted service.
If existing CRM automation already solves the problem, consider an integration/template or stop.
Any unsafe external action pauses sending immediately.
At pilot end publish an honest decision memo:what worked, what failed, which claim is supported andwhether to build, change or stop.
