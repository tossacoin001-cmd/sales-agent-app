# Roadmap and launch

## Phases
| Phase | Planning estimate | Output | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | 1–2 weeks | Ten interviews; segment decision | Recurring evidenced pain and committed participants |
| Prototype | Several focused days | Five-user task/comprehension test | 4/5 complete core task; no critical misunderstanding |
| Concierge | 14-day baseline + 30-day assisted period; renewal at billing date | Measured use, accuracy, cost and payment | Pilot gate in document 03 |
| Private MVP | 4–6 engineering weeks after scope confirmed | Isolated multi-user application | Reliability, permissions and value criteria |
| Paid beta | 4–8 weeks, adjusted to sales cycle | Bounded cohort and renewal evidence | Supportable cost and operating reliability |
| Wider release | Conditional | Repeatable onboarding/support | Claims and capacity justified by evidence |

These are estimates, not delivery promises. Team capacity, provider reviews and findings can extend the schedule. No application build is requested now.

## Release slices
M0: auth, workspace roles, private goals, consent and manual records.
M1: one read integration, source events, incremental sync and stale indicators.
M2: pipeline, top-three decision queue, editable drafts and manual completion.
M3: exact approvals, outbox, dispatch/reconciliation, suppression and budgets.
M4: receipt basis, allocations, subscription entitlements, export and deletion.
M5: monitoring, restore drill, abuse tests, role audit and invited beta.

Do not expose dispatch before its material failure tests pass.

## Defer
Automatic WhatsApp, Microsoft 365, ads, custom CRM replacement, universal bank access, native mobile, agency white labelling, a marketplace and app-building agents.

Add a feature when evidence shows a recurring job that simpler workflows cannot solve. Competitor feature lists are not our backlog.

## First-sight positioning experiment
Hero: **Know where your sales goal stands—and what to do next.**
Subtext: **Turn permitted enquiries, follow-ups and payment records into a short daily plan.**
Primary CTA: **Try a guided sales review.**
Secondary CTA: **See a sample day.**

Within 45 seconds: goal → honest gap → overdue buyer request → evidence → editable action → confirmed outcome. Label synthetic examples as demos. Show approval and channel limits clearly.

Five-second test: can a target buyer explain the value without help? Does “cash” mean actual receipts to them? Do they understand what they still control? Improve based on observed misunderstanding, not founder preference alone.

No money guarantee, invented testimonial, fake scarcity or unapproved logo.

## Acquisition sequence
Use permission-based founder relationships and relevant business communities in the chosen cohort. Interview/pilot invitations are research offers, not an excuse to reuse suppressed service prospects.

Publish a useful walkthrough. Publish a real case only with consent and precise claims. Distinguish time saved from revenue created.

Measure qualified visit → understood value → pilot request → activation → paid renewal. Do not invent a “market-standard” conversion rate.

No paid advertising until retention, economics and operating capacity justify a separate spending decision. This planning task authorises no spend.

## Claims ladder
Demo: organises example data.
Pilot: assisted process helped a specified cohort on a stated measure.
Beta: software performs a bounded workflow with documented reliability.
Repeatable commercial value: paid retention and measured useful outcomes across a defined period.
Revenue uplift: needs suitable comparison and attribution; not all receipts belong to the product.

## Launch evidence
Provider permissions; required verification; privacy/merchant review; tenant tests; ambiguous-send recovery; account revocation; financial reconciliation; restore drill; incident owner; support capacity; accessible mobile flow; billing/refund policies; truthful claims; rate and spend controls.
