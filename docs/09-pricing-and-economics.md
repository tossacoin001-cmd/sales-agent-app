# Pricing and unit economics — hypotheses

## What customers pay for
Useful decisions, saved time and reliable workflow coverage. Do not sell safety as an upgrade or charge by how many unsolicited messages the app can send.

Proposed interview/pilot anchors:
- Solo: ₦15,000/month, one owner, one active goal and bounded review/draft use.
- Small team: ₦35,000/month, shared pipeline, handoffs and a larger bounded allowance.

These are unvalidated test prices, not approved launch prices. Set quotas from measured costs. Disclose human-assisted onboarding or concierge fees separately. Do not assume a Nigerian subscription price converts directly into a global price.

Approved Toss service offers at ₦50k/₦150k/₦350k remain distinct from software subscriptions.

## Direct cost model
Monthly direct cost/account = model usage + research + integration fees + storage/compute allocation + support labour + payment fees + refund/abuse allowance.

Gross margin = (subscription revenue − direct cost) / subscription revenue.

Record founder support time at a shadow hourly rate. Report fixed engineering/security assessment and acquisition cost separately, with any amortisation assumptions. No real SaaS infrastructure or support bill was measured for this plan.

Illustrative assumptions, NGN:
| Scenario | Price | Direct cost | Gross margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Solo, light | 15,000 | 3,500 | 76.67% |
| Solo, heavy | 15,000 | 13,500 | 10.00% |
| Team, light | 35,000 | 8,000 | 77.14% |
| Team, heavy | 35,000 | 23,000 | 34.29% |

Only light assumptions pass the proposed 75% planning gate. This is sensitivity analysis, not proof of profitability. A labour-heavy concierge pilot may lose money at these prices; classify that as research spending or disclose an assisted-service fee.

## Controls
Use incremental sync, cached public evidence with expiry, bounded research per decision and deterministic rules before model calls. Cap run steps, tenant spend and expensive tools.

Track actual token, provider and support use without raw private content. Mark estimated costs until reconciled. A quota limit can pause more research while preserving viewing, export and account control.

No surprise charges for endless agent loops. The billing page distinguishes subscription, provider usage and assisted services.

## Billing behaviour
Entitlements follow verified subscription/payment events rather than a checkout success screen. Test signed webhooks, retries, duplicate events, renewal, expiry, past due, grace periods, cancellations, refunds and disputes.

Past due may pause dispatch but does not erase contact history or suppressions. Preserve access/export according to the stated policy.

The provider and merchant eligibility remain unconfirmed. Check country, currency, tax, settlement and refund support before selection.

## Commercial evidence
Track paid activation, meaningful weekly use, renewal, gross margin, support minutes, recommendation usefulness and evidenced completion.

Break-even accounts = fixed monthly cost / contribution per account, only when inputs are measured. Do not turn hypothetical users into forecast MRR. Publish retention only with cohort dates, denominators and exclusions.
