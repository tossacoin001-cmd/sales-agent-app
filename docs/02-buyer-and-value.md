# Buyer, value and market position
## Why someone might pay
Owners usually do not buy a goal field or an AI summary. They may pay when the product:
- Surfaces a real unanswered buyer or overdue quote before it is forgotten.
- Gives a short, relevant next reply using the actual conversation.
- Makes responsibility and next decision clear across a small team.
- Reconciles cash progress without counting promises or screenshots.
- Explains where work is stuck and suggests one testable intervention.
- Saves recurring review and follow-up time without creating extra work.
Each benefit must be measured in the pilot; none is assumed proven.

## Initial job to be done
“When I have enquiries and outstanding quotes across email and manual notes, help me decide what deserves attention today, respond consistently and see actual progress towards my target.”

Choose one initial cohort:
- Primary hypothesis: owner-led digital/service agencies,2–10people, existing quote pipeline, email use and meaningful deal values.
- Secondary hypothesis: event/photography/commercial-service owners with existing inbound enquiries and permission to provide records.
- Later: salons, retail/social sellers and other WhatsApp-first teams after official channel support and a matching workflow are validated.

Do not mix cohorts and report an average as universal evidence. Record segment, channel mix, lead volume, offer economics and existing tooling separately.

## Three modes, one foundation
1. **Existing opportunity mode, first:** unresolved enquiries, quotes, renewals and agreed customer follow-ups. A customer request or actual quote is stronger context than a guessed cold need.
2. **Acquisition experiment mode, later:** research and personalised outreach only after fit, source, permission and deliverability gates; no bulk scrape-to-send feature.
3. **Retention mode, later:** reviews, rebooking and referral reminders with appropriate consent and channel rules.

Owner business goals may include verified cash, signed scopes, appointments or qualified conversations. Each needs its own evidence definition. Do not combine them into a single “growth score.”

## What makes this better than a spreadsheet plus ChatGPT?
A spreadsheet can hold targets and tasks. The paid product must continuously reconcile source events, remember contact rules, detect conflicting records, keep owners accountable for selected actions and prove time saved. If customers get equal value from a simple spreadsheet and a weekly call, sell the assisted service or template first; don't build a SaaS on a weak incremental benefit.

## Current competitive reality
Research checked27October2026UTC:
- HubSpot documents pipeline revenue, target attainment, sales analytics and coaching. Goal tracking/reporting alone is not unique.
- Apollo documents AI prospect research and context-aware sales outreach. AI-written personalised emails alone are not unique.
- Clay documents enrichment and AI/data workflows syncing to CRMs. Research automation alone is not unique.
- HighLevel lists CRM, workflows, conversations, booking, reporting and marketing. Its published base monthly tiers are USD97/297/497; AI has additional pricing. A “business platform with agents” is already a crowded category.
See source register. Vendor pages establish advertised capabilities, not customer satisfaction, our superiority or demand for Toss.

## Differentiation hypothesis
A narrow owner experience: **cash-goal clarity + evidence-linked next actions + safe execution + explicit learning from actual outcomes**, with small-team workflows and local-currency onboarding.

The distinction is UX, trust, workflow fit and measurable usefulness—not an unsupported claim that competitors cannot do it. Interview customers who already use CRMs: what still needs daily manual review? A CRM integration or companion may win more trust than a replacement.

## Relationship to Visibility OS
Avoid two products both claiming to run a person's whole business. Candidate boundary:
Visibility OS = learning, planning and visibility routines.
Revenue Execution = evidence-linked sales opportunities and decisions.
Shared auth, workspaces, notifications and billing if a repository audit supports it. Product naming, domain and separation remain decisions to validate; do not launch a second dashboard simply to create another subscription.
